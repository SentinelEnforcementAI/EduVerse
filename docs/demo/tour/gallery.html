Platform tour · design-partner MVP

Safeguarding intelligence for UK schools and trusts.

Sentinel Watch reads the data schools already hold — attendance, behaviour, attainment, pastoral — connects the dots a busy DSL cannot, and surfaces the child who needs attention, with the reasoning attached, days before the next scheduled meeting would have caught it. The system flags; a human always decides. Every screen below is running software against a seeded database — not mockups, and not a real child.

Human-in-the-loop

The system flags. A person always decides. Enforced in schema and code, not copy.

UK data residency

All data and inference stays in AWS London. No pupil data leaves UK infrastructure.

Explainability

Every signal carries its reasoning — which rule fired, on which data points.

Full audit

Every read and write against a child’s record is logged. Append-only. Soft-delete only.

Overlay-first

Reads from source systems via Wonde. Never writes back to the school’s MIS.

The core loop

A signal, its reasoning, and a human decision

Everything funnels to this screen. The risk engine flags a pattern; the DSL sees exactly why, with every underlying data point; and the DSL — never the system — decides what happens next.

Trust command

The whole trust on one screen

A Director of Safeguarding oversees every school in the trust — with cross-school pattern intelligence no single school can see. The role boundary is enforced at the database with row-level security, not hidden in the UI.

The school desk

Everything a DSL works from

A school DSL sees only their own school. The desk brings the concern queue, the case file, the document library and the compliance surfaces into one place.

Email mission control

No concern lost in an inbox

Safeguarding mail is captured, auto-threaded onto the right case where it matches, and everything else lands in an intake queue for a human to place — nothing slips through the gaps. Outbound is drafted by the DSL and sent from the case; the platform never sends anything itself.

Safeguarding operations

Alerts, governance, audit, out-of-hours

The surrounding operational surfaces — how serious signals reach a person, how the trust assures itself, and the append-only record underneath it all.

Commercialisation · administration

Ready to onboard, meter, and tune per customer

The commercialisation layer that turns the product into something a trust can be sold, set up on, and billed for — self-serve for a trust administrator, all audited.

Access

Invite-only, magic-link sign-in

There is no open sign-up. Accounts are provisioned; sign-in is an emailed magic link, structured so school SSO can replace it later without a schema change.
